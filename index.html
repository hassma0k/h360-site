FuelMoveRestFast

Your whole day — Fuel, Move, Rest, Fast — in one calm Arabic-and-English app that judges you by the week, not the day.

  • Daily targets, weekly judgement.A missed day is never a failure: H360 shows a calm catch-up for the rest of the week.
  • Snap it or say it.Log a meal with a photo, an Arabic or English voice note or a line of text. Fast estimates you can adjust — even kabsa.
  • One calm score every morning.Readiness 0–100 against your own baseline, with an Apple Watch (or another device) that records sleep and overnight heart rate in Apple Health.
  • Your fast counts itself.Fasting hours come from the gaps between your meals.
  • Every workout, analysed.From your Watch, Garmin or any app that writes workouts to Apple Health: zone minutes, recovery and your week.
  • Better with friends. Private by design.Your raw health data stays on your iPhone. Only four daily totals are shared, and only when you join a challenge or add friends.
